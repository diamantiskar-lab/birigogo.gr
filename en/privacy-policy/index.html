Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how BIRIGOGO (hereinafter "BIRIGOGO" or the "Company") processes personal information and personal data collected in relation to you when you communicate with the Company in the context of an existing or potential future cooperation.

We encourage you to read this Privacy Policy carefully.

The Company, with registered address at 65 Agiou Meletiou Street, Athens, 11251, Attica, Greece, complies with the General Data Protection Regulation, Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR"), adopted by the European Parliament and the Council on 27 April 2016.

1. About BIRIGOGO

BIRIGOGO creates interactive educational applications inspired by Ancient Greek drama. Its educational applications are addressed to young people, children, parents, students and educators, with the aim of encouraging engagement with Ancient Greek tragedy, comedy and mythology through simple touch interactions, visual storytelling, illustration, narration, music and sound design.

2. What is Personal Data?

Personal Data means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, such as a person's name, postal address, email address, telephone number or other contact details.

3. What is the Processing of Personal Data?

Processing means any operation or set of operations performed on Personal Data, whether or not by automated means. Such operations may include collection, recording, organisation, structuring, storage, adaptation, alteration, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure, transmission, dissemination, combination, restriction, deletion or destruction.

4. What Personal Data Does the Company Process?

We take care to process only the Personal Data that is strictly necessary, appropriate and relevant for the purposes for which it is collected. Regarding information collected through cookies on our website, please refer to our Cookie Policy.

Within this context, BIRIGOGO may process:

  • identification and contact details, such as your first and last name, postal address and contact information, when you enter into a contractual or professional relationship with BIRIGOGO;
  • information that you voluntarily provide when contacting us through our website, email or other means of communication.

The BIRIGOGO applications themselves do not collect personal information from users unless this is expressly stated within a specific application or required by a third-party platform used by the user.

5. Why Do We Process Your Personal Data?

The Company processes Personal Data for the following purposes:

  • to comply with obligations imposed by applicable legal and regulatory requirements;
  • to respond to questions, requests or messages submitted through our contact form or other communication channels;
  • to manage contractual, professional or commercial relationships;
  • to protect the legitimate interests and legal rights of the Company.

6. Will the Company Process Your Data for Other Purposes?

The Company will not process your Personal Data for purposes other than those described above, unless required or permitted by law or unless you have been informed accordingly.

7. What is the Legal Basis for Processing?

Your Personal Data may be processed on the basis of:

  • the Company's legal obligations, including tax and accounting obligations;

8. Who May Receive Your Personal Data?

Access to your Personal Data may be granted to authorised employees or partners of BIRIGOGO who are bound by confidentiality obligations. Our processors may include technology providers, application developers, website designers, hosting providers, technical support providers and other service providers that assist the Company with its software, websites, products, services and communications.

BIRIGOGO does not intentionally transfer Personal Data outside the European Union unless such a transfer is necessary. BIRIGOGO is committed to protecting your Personal Data.
